# 01 — Context & Strategic Objectives

## Academic Context

This project was completed as part of a **Professional Master's in Integrated QSE Management (Quality–Safety–Environment)** during the 2019–2020 academic year.

The applied study focused on the **initiation of a Quality Management System according to ISO 9001:2015** within an anonymized service-sector organization.

The project combined academic methodology with an applied analysis of organizational context, processes, risks and quality-management practices.

## Problematic

The central question was:

> **How can an organization establish the foundations of a structured QMS while aligning organizational context, stakeholder expectations, operational processes, risks, quality objectives and continuous improvement?**

The challenge was therefore not limited to documenting ISO requirements. It was to translate those requirements into a practical management framework.

## Main Objective

Assess existing quality-management practices, identify gaps against ISO 9001:2015 and propose a structured plan for initiating a QMS.

## Specific Objectives

1. Understand the organizational context.
2. Identify relevant interested parties.
3. Analyze internal and external factors.
4. Identify and map key processes.
5. Analyze risks and opportunities.
6. Review existing quality practices.
7. Assess the principal ISO 9001:2015 requirement areas.
8. Identify gaps and improvement priorities.
9. Define corrective and improvement actions.
10. Establish responsibilities and follow-up mechanisms.
11. Propose a structured implementation roadmap.

## Strategic Dimension

The project connects quality management with organizational strategy:

**Strategic Diagnosis → Strategic Objectives → Strategic Choices → Operational Deployment → Performance Monitoring**

This positioning treats the QMS as a management system supporting organizational performance rather than only as a collection of procedures.

## Scope

The scope covers **assessment and initiation** of a QMS.

It does not claim completion of implementation, certification, accredited auditing or a formal certification-readiness conclusion.

## Expected Contribution

The project establishes a structured bridge between:

**Strategy → Processes → Risks → ISO Requirements → Actions → Governance → Continuous Improvement**
