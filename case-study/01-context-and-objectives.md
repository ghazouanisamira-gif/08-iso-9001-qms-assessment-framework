# 01 — Context & Objectives

## 1. Academic Context

This final-year Master's project explored the **initiation of a Quality Management System according to ISO 9001:2015** within an anonymized service-sector organization.

The project was developed as an applied academic study: ISO 9001 requirements were considered alongside the organization's operational reality, processes, stakeholders, risks and existing quality practices.

The objective was to move beyond a purely theoretical reading of the standard and translate its principles into a structured improvement and implementation approach.

## 2. Project Problematic

The central question was:

> **How can an organization structure the foundations of a Quality Management System aligned with ISO 9001:2015 while connecting quality requirements to operational processes, risks, responsibilities and measurable improvement?**

This question led to a diagnostic and project-oriented approach rather than a simple clause-by-clause review.

## 3. Main Objective

The main objective was to assess the existing quality-management practices, identify gaps and improvement opportunities against ISO 9001:2015, and propose a structured approach for progressively establishing a QMS.

## 4. Specific Objectives

The project aimed to:

1. understand the organizational context;
2. identify relevant interested parties;
3. determine the key processes and their interactions;
4. identify risks and opportunities;
5. review existing quality practices and documented information;
6. assess alignment with ISO 9001:2015 requirement areas;
7. identify gaps and potential nonconformities;
8. prioritize improvement needs;
9. define actions and responsibilities;
10. establish an implementation roadmap;
11. introduce monitoring and governance mechanisms.

## 5. Scope

The study covers the assessment and initiation stages of a QMS:

**Context → Diagnosis → Assessment → Prioritization → Planning**

It does not present a completed certification project.

## 6. Expected Contribution

The expected contribution was to provide a practical bridge between:

- ISO 9001 requirements;
- organizational processes;
- operational risks;
- quality performance;
- responsibilities;
- improvement actions;
- project governance.

## 7. Deliverable Logic

The project follows the sequence:

**Understand → Diagnose → Map → Assess → Prioritize → Plan → Improve**

This logic remains applicable to future QMS, operational excellence and transformation initiatives.
