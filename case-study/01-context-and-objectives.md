# 01 — Context & Objectives

## 1. Academic Context

The project was completed as a final-year study within a **Professional Master's in Integrated QSE Management (Quality–Safety–Environment)** during the 2019–2020 academic year.

The work focused on the **initiation of a Quality Management System according to ISO 9001:2015** within an anonymized service-sector organization.

The project combined academic requirements with an applied analysis of organizational practices, processes, risks and quality-management mechanisms.

## 2. Problematic

The central problematic was to determine how an organization could establish the foundations of a structured QMS while ensuring alignment between:

- organizational context;
- stakeholder expectations;
- operational processes;
- quality objectives;
- risks and opportunities;
- responsibilities;
- performance monitoring;
- continuous improvement.

## 3. QQOQCP Framing

The project subject was structured through the QQOQCP method:

| Question | Project interpretation |
|---|---|
| **Qui ?** | Management, process owners, quality actors, managers, operational teams and relevant stakeholders |
| **Quoi ?** | Initiation of a QMS according to ISO 9001:2015 |
| **Où ?** | Anonymized service-sector organization |
| **Quand ?** | Academic year 2019–2020 |
| **Comment ?** | Context analysis, process approach, risk analysis, ISO self-assessment and action planning |
| **Pourquoi ?** | Structure quality practices and establish a framework for improvement |

## 4. Main Objective

Assess the existing quality-management practices, identify gaps against ISO 9001:2015 and propose a structured plan for initiating a QMS.

## 5. Specific Objectives

1. Understand the organizational context.
2. Identify interested parties.
3. Analyze internal and external factors.
4. Identify and map key processes.
5. Analyze risks and opportunities.
6. Review existing quality practices.
7. Assess the principal ISO 9001:2015 requirements.
8. Identify gaps and priorities.
9. Define improvement actions.
10. Establish responsibilities and follow-up.
11. Propose a structured implementation roadmap.

## 6. Strategic Dimension

The project links quality management with strategic management:

**Strategic Diagnosis → Strategic Objectives → Strategic Choices → Operational Deployment → Performance Monitoring**

This ensures that the QMS is considered as a management system supporting organizational performance, rather than only as a collection of procedures.

## 7. Scope

The scope covers the **assessment and initiation** of a QMS.

It does not claim:
- completion of implementation;
- successful certification;
- accredited audit activity;
- formal certification readiness.

## 8. Expected Contribution

The expected contribution is a structured bridge between:

**Strategy → Processes → Risks → ISO Requirements → Actions → Governance → Continuous Improvement**
