# ISO 9001 Clause Mapping

## Purpose

This framework connects ISO 9001:2015 requirement areas with evidence, current state, identified gaps, risks, actions and follow-up.

It creates traceability between the standard and the implementation program.

## Mapping Model

**Requirement → Evidence → Current State → Gap → Risk / Priority → Action → Follow-up**

## Clause 4 — Context of the Organization

Key themes:
- context;
- interested parties;
- QMS scope;
- processes and interactions.

## Clause 5 — Leadership

Key themes:
- leadership and commitment;
- quality policy;
- roles;
- responsibilities;
- authorities.

## Clause 6 — Planning

Key themes:
- risks and opportunities;
- quality objectives;
- planning for changes.

## Clause 7 — Support

Key themes:
- resources;
- competence;
- awareness;
- communication;
- documented information.

## Clause 8 — Operation

Key themes:
- operational planning and control;
- customer requirements;
- service/product provision;
- externally provided processes.

## Clause 9 — Performance Evaluation

Key themes:
- monitoring and measurement;
- customer satisfaction;
- internal audit;
- management review.

## Clause 10 — Improvement

Key themes:
- nonconformity;
- corrective action;
- continual improvement.
