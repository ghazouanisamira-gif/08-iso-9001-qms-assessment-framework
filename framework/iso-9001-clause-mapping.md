# ISO 9001 Clause Mapping

## Purpose

A structured mapping method connecting ISO 9001:2015 requirement areas with available evidence, current state, identified gaps and improvement actions.

## Mapping Structure

| ISO 9001 Area | Requirement | Evidence | Current State | Gap | Risk / Priority | Action | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 — Context | Context, interested parties, scope, processes | Evidence | Assessed | Identified | Prioritized | Defined | Monitored |
| 5 — Leadership | Leadership, policy, responsibilities | Evidence | Assessed | Identified | Prioritized | Defined | Monitored |
| 6 — Planning | Risks, opportunities, objectives, changes | Evidence | Assessed | Identified | Prioritized | Defined | Monitored |
| 7 — Support | Resources, competence, communication, documentation | Evidence | Assessed | Identified | Prioritized | Defined | Monitored |
| 8 — Operation | Operational planning and service provision | Evidence | Assessed | Identified | Prioritized | Defined | Monitored |
| 9 — Performance Evaluation | Monitoring, satisfaction, audit, review | Evidence | Assessed | Identified | Prioritized | Defined | Monitored |
| 10 — Improvement | Nonconformity, corrective action, improvement | Evidence | Assessed | Identified | Prioritized | Defined | Monitored |

## Use

The mapping supports traceability between:

**Requirement → Evidence → Diagnosis → Gap → Risk → Action → Follow-up**

> This is an academic project-analysis framework, not a certification checklist or formal audit conclusion.
